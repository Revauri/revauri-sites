## One-line promise

An AI employee for the job you hate — we build the workflow, we run it, you stay the boss. Much cheaper than hiring a person.

## Who it is for

Local service owners: roofers, clinics, shops, contractors, and similar.

They are busy running the business. They do not want another dashboard. They want a hire for a painful, annoying, or time-eating job.

## The offer (one package to start)

**Name:** Revauri AI Digital Hire

Do not launch three tiers yet. Sell this one until 3–5 clients are live.

### What they buy

1. **Name the job** — the pain, annoyance, or busywork sitting on the owner.
2. **Build the workflow** — Joseph designs and installs the system around how their business actually runs.
3. **Run the hire** — Revauri manages it every month. The owner approves anything customer-facing.

### First systems we usually install

Included in the standard quote: **one primary workflow + one supporting workflow.**

- Lead / quote follow-up
- Missed-call or after-hours capture
- Review requests and review replies
- Inbox / appointment busywork
- Another custom workflow if that is the real mess

The front door is “AI employee.” The delivery is a custom workflow we create and manage.

## What is not included

- A new website or redesign (quoted separately)
- Ads management
- Sending emails, texts, or posts without approval
- Medical, legal, pricing, or licensed professional advice
- Replacing a real receptionist, tech, or office manager
- Unlimited new workflows
- Anything that regularly needs more than ~90 minutes a week

---

## How we keep “every client is different” from sinking the price

The **$1,749 + $895** quote is for a normal local-service job: two workflows, about 90 minutes a week.

If the look shows a heavier mess — more systems, more locations, more weekly work — **do not start at the standard price.** Stop and quote before setup:

| If we find… | Do this |
|---|---|
| 1–2 workflows, normal weekly run | Standard quote |
| 3rd workflow they also need | +$750 setup + $295/mo |
| Clearly bigger than 90 min/week | Custom quote before any build |
| Website is the real leak | Separate website quote |

Never say “unlimited” or “we’ll just figure it out.” Name the two jobs in writing before setup money is taken.
